## 📖 Overview

**1688.com (阿里巴巴批发网)** is Alibaba Group's premier domestic B2B marketplace. It is the real, direct manufacturing engine behind Alibaba.com, AliExpress, Temu, Shein, Taobao, and Amazon FBA — hosting over **10 million Chinese factories, raw manufacturers, and industrial suppliers**.

However, extracting data from 1688 has historically been a massive headache for Western brands and sourcing teams:
- Mandatory Chinese mobile number (+86) SMS verification and QR code login walls.
- Dynamic Alibaba mtop token encryption (`_m_h5_tk`, `tfstk`) and anti-bot challenges.
- Chinese character GBK encoding that corrupts URLs and keyword search terms.
- Complex nested variation trees and pricing tiers hidden behind authenticated AJAX calls.

**1688 Wholesale Scraper by UnitBytes** completely solves these procurement roadblocks. Powered by an autonomous extraction engine, this Actor extracts up to **10,000 items per run** with **10 parallel workers** — delivering clean, spreadsheet-ready factory data into Excel, CSV, or Google Sheets with zero account or cookie setup needed.

## ✨ Key Features

- **🏭 4 Specialized Wholesale Sourcing Channels:** Query specialized 1688 channels at the source:
  - `找工厂` (Direct Factory / OEM & ODM) — filters out middlemen and trading agents.
  - `超级工厂` (Super Factory) — restricted to Alibaba's elite certified mega-plants.
  - `跨境专供` (Cross-Border Export) — tailored for Amazon FBA, Temu, and TikTok Shop sellers with CE/FCC/RoHS compliance.
  - `现货批发` (Ready-to-Ship Spot Market) — in-stock items ready to ship in 24–48 hours.
- **💰 Tiered Bulk Pricing Brackets:** Captures the full volume price schedule (e.g. 2–99 pcs, 100–499 pcs, 500+ pcs) for accurate cost modeling and supplier negotiation.
- **📦 Amazon FBA Packaging Intelligence:** Extracts physical package weights in grams (`weightGrams`) and physical dimensions (`lengthCm`, `widthCm`, `heightCm`, `volumeCm3`) per individual SKU variant to accurately calculate international freight and FBA fulfillment fees.
- **🎨 Complete SKU Matrix with Spec Photos:** Collects every variation (color, size, packaging) along with variant-specific pricing, real-time stock levels, and matching high-res product photos.
- **🛡️ Factory Due Diligence Badges:** Verify manufacturing capability instantly with **Super Factory (超级工厂)** flags, **SGS / BV third-party on-site audits (深度验厂)**, and repeat customer **Repurchase Rates (回头率)**.
- **🔄 Stateful Resumption Engine (`resumptionToken`):** Checkpoints pagination state so you can resume multi-page scrapes seamlessly with **0 duplicate products across runs**.
- **⚡ High Concurrency & Speed:** 10 parallel workers extract thousands of listings without crashing or slowing down.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Do I need a 1688 account, Alipay, or Chinese phone number?
**No.** The scraper operates entirely autonomously without requiring an account, phone number (+86), or session cookies.

### Can I search using English keywords?
**Yes.** Standard English terms (e.g. `phone case`, `yoga pants`, `hoodie`, `earbuds`, `smart watch`) are supported. Sourcing teams often also search with native Chinese product terms (e.g. `手机壳`, `运动裤`, `蓝牙耳机`) for maximum supplier discovery.

### How do I use the Amazon FBA packaging weights and dimensions?
Every record in **Full Detail** mode includes the physical `weightGrams` and dimensions (`lengthCm`, `widthCm`, `heightCm`) per variant. Use these numbers directly in your Amazon FBA revenue calculator to determine exact shipping and fulfillment fees.

### What is the difference between Super Factory and Factory Inspection?
- **Super Factory (超级工厂):** Alibaba's highest verified certification tier awarded to large-scale manufacturers with substantial annual revenue, modern production lines, and direct factory management.
- **Factory Inspection (深度验厂):** Facilities audited physically on-site by accredited third-party inspection firms (such as SGS or Bureau Veritas) to verify worker counts, factory square footage, and production machinery.

### How does pagination resumption work?
Every exported item and run summary includes an encrypted **`resumptionToken`**. If you want to continue a massive scrape without re-extracting previous items, simply pass the token into your next run's input.
